Both upstream fonts were converted to WOFF2 using fonttools 4.60.1 and
brotli 1.2.0, without subsetting glyphs or changing family names:
  python -m fontTools.ttLib.woff2 compress <source.ttf> -o <output.woff2>

These committed assets are copied from public/fonts into the frontend bundle
by Vite and embedded by Tauri on every platform. Neither application startup
nor CI needs to download or convert fonts. Keep both licenses with the fonts.

Typography: Manrope for Latin text/numerals, Noto Sans SC for Chinese text;
system fonts cover glyphs outside these fonts. Headings and primary controls
use Bold (700); descriptions stay Regular (400), secondary labels Medium (500).
